# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

DevTechx Labs welcomes participation across identities, backgrounds, abilities,
and experience levels. Everyone deserves dignity and fair treatment.

## Encouraged Behaviors

Be considerate, credit others, respect differing perspectives, accept feedback,
take responsibility, and help repair harm.

## Restricted Behaviors

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, threats,
disclosure of private information, impersonation, and disruptive promotion are unacceptable.

A confidential reporting contact has not yet been published. Do not post sensitive
incident details in public issues. Organization owners must appoint an enforcement
owner and publish a private reporting route before inviting community participation.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

Appointed moderators review reports confidentially, consider impact and context,
and seek appropriate repair. Responses may include a warning, limited participation,
temporary suspension, or permanent removal. Serious incidents may require immediate
removal. Moderators with a conflict must recuse themselves.

## Scope

These expectations cover DevTechx Labs community spaces and official representation.
